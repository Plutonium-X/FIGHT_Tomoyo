# Tomoyo and the Underground City

This mod begins in the small hidden crypt containing a lich near the entrance to the city.

A new passage in the crypt will take you to an underground "city", where you can immediately meet and recruit <a href="https://clannad.fandom.com/wiki/Tomoyo_Sakagami">Tomoyo Sakagami</a>, a heroine from Clannad and its sequels. (A level 7 Fighter, ridiculously overpowered with +1 APR and +14 to THAC0.) However, you don't have to keep her in your party to continue. She is mute and has no impact on the rest of the adventure.

The adventure then takes you through a series of interconnected maps, each filled with hundreds (thousands?) of monsters that grant no XP but drop hundreds of powerful items (+4/+5), as well as some outrageously overpowered equipment (a returning +3 throwing dagger dealing 1d30+3 damage, a cloak that regenerates 5 HP per second, and unlimited-ammunition crossbows dealing 2d60 damage).
